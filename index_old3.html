Two days in the Andes

Every step takes you somewhere extraordinary.

A demanding descent, a night in the oasis and an early-morning climb rewarded by the first light of day.

Day 01 · The descent

Into the canyon

15 km · Approximately 7 hours of hiking
  • 3:00–3:30 a.m. — Pickup at your accommodation in Arequipa.
  • Breakfast in Chivay after approximately three hours of travel.
  • Cruz del Cóndor viewpoint at approximately 3,600 m, with time to look for condors and take photographs.
  • Transfer to San Miguel at 3,200 m and begin the descent.
  • Cross the bridge and reach San Juan de Chuccho at 2,230 m for lunch with a local family.
  • Continue hiking to Sangalle Oasis at 1,900 m. Enjoy the natural surroundings and swimming pools, then stay overnight.
Day 02 · The ascent

Rise with the Andes

7 km · Approximately 3–4 hours of hiking
  • 4:30 a.m. — Begin the ascent in the dark and watch the canyon illuminate at sunrise.
  • Reach Cabanaconde at approximately 3,300 m and have breakfast.
  • Travel by vehicle and stop at Antahuilque viewpoint to see the agricultural terraces.
  • Visit the thermal baths; entrance is paid separately.
  • Continue to Chivay for lunch, which is not included.
  • Stop at the volcanoes viewpoint at approximately 4,190 m, then return to Arequipa around 5:30–6:00 p.m.

Frequently asked questions
How much does the trek cost?

The advertised price is S/ 270 per person for the 2-day, 1-night tour. Colca Canyon entrance fees, thermal baths and the final lunch in Chivay are additional.

What time does the tour start and finish?

Pickup in Arequipa is between 3:00 and 3:30 a.m. The expected return to Arequipa is between 5:30 and 6:00 p.m. on the second day.

How challenging is the hike?

The route involves a long descent on day one and a steep ascent starting around 4:30 a.m. on day two. Prepare for strenuous hiking, altitude changes and uneven terrain.

Are meals and accommodation included?

One night at Sangalle Oasis, two continental breakfasts, one conventional lunch and one dinner are included according to the owner's information. The final lunch in Chivay is not included.

How do I confirm my reservation?

Full advance payment is required. The booking is confirmed after payment. Use the operator's verified contact or booking link once those details have been added to this website.

What should I bring?

Ask the operator for a final packing list. In general, prepare hiking footwear, layers for cold early mornings, sun protection, water and a small backpack. Confirm the expected weather and equipment requirements before departure.
